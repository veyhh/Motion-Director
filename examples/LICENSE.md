The `humanoid.glb` fixture is an original procedural mannequin created for Motion Director.
No external assets, textures, animation data, or downloaded meshes are used.
It is covered by the repository's [MIT license](../LICENSE).

Reproducible source: `motion_director/blender/fixture.py`.
It contains one 19-bone armature and a vertex-weighted mesh, including a forward-facing visor.
Rigid segment weights make motion and bone mapping visible; this is not a substitute for
testing organic shoulder deformation on production character skinning.
